# The unified Home: Today, Continue and the menu

When Alexandria shows all your servers together (the unified Home), the
page opens on **Today**: a short look at what you are in the middle of,
what is coming out and what is new, before the shelves of each server. It
is the same on phones, tablets, the Mac and Windows.

(New in the app update after phone build 1273. Before it, the page was
titled "Unified Library" and opened straight onto the shelves.)

## The top of the page

- The **menu button** (three lines) at the top left opens the side menu.
- Today's date and the word **Today**.
- Your **profile circle** at the top right, in your profile color. Tap it
  to go to the **You** tab (in the older home layouts it opens Settings).
- On the Mac and Windows there is also a **Refresh** button. On a phone or
  tablet, pull the page down to refresh.

### The stat pills

Under the title, up to three pills:

- **time this week** — listening, reading and watching in the last 7 days;
- **day streak** — your current streak, the same one the stats page shows;
- **finished** — books, films and episodes finished in the last 7 days.

A pill with nothing to show is left out. Tap any pill to open your stats
and goals. To hide the pills, hold one, or turn off **Stats** in
**Arrange** (see below); turn it back on there.

The numbers come from what this device has recorded; nothing is fetched
to show them.

### All · Listen · Read · Watch · Music

These tabs filter the **whole page** — the Continue cards, the cards under
them and every shelf:

- **Listen**: audiobooks and podcasts;
- **Read**: ebooks and comics;
- **Watch**: films, shows and episodes;
- **Music**: albums and songs.

The app remembers the last tab you picked on this device. If a tab has
nothing on it yet, the page says so.

## Continue

**Continue** shows the things you were last in the middle of, as large
cards with the cover, what it is (Audiobook, Ebook, Film, Episode …), how
far you are and what is left (for example "63% · 4h 10m left").

- **How many cards: 1, 3 or 5** (3 to start with). Pick it in **⋯** (next
  to Hide) or in **Arrange**, under the Continue switch ("Cards shown").
  The app remembers it on this device.
- The cards are the things you touched **most recently, from any
  service** — with 3, your three most recent, even if two are from the
  same server.
- With **1** you get one wide card and nothing to swipe. With **3** or
  **5**, **swipe** sideways for the next card; the edge of the next one
  peeks in, and the dots underneath show where you are.
- On a tablet or a computer the cards sit side by side and fill the page
  from edge to edge, lined up with the cards and shelves below: 2 across
  on a narrower window or an iPad in portrait, 3 or more on a wide one.
  When they all fit there is nothing to swipe and no dots. With **5** on a
  window that fits fewer, the dots (or a swipe) bring the rest in, and the
  last card still ends at the page's right edge.
- The **colored circles** on the right of "Continue" are your services
  (one letter each, in the service's color). **Tap a circle** to see only
  that service — its 1, 3 or 5 most recent items; tap it again to see
  every service.
- **Resume** carries on where you left off. Tap the card itself for its
  details page, or hold it for more actions.
- Tap **Continue ›** for everything you are in the middle of, as a grid.
- To hide the cards, tap **⋯** › **Hide**; **Show** brings them back. You
  can also turn **Continue** off and on in **Arrange**.

## The cards under Continue

Each card shows only when it has something to show, and follows the tab
you picked:

- **Also in progress** — up to three more things you are partway through.
  Anything already on a Continue card is left out, so nothing shows twice.
- **Coming this week** — up to three releases from **Upcoming releases**
  that come out within the next 7 days (new episodes, films, albums and
  books from your libraries). If nothing comes out this week, it shows
  the next ones within a month as **Coming soon**. **All** opens Upcoming
  releases. Home only shows what Upcoming releases already found; it does
  not check for new releases itself. See
  [Upcoming releases](upcoming-releases.md).
- **Your Librarian suggests** — only when you have set up an AI server for
  the Librarian (Settings › AI) and Kids mode is off. Once a day the
  Librarian picks 2 or 3 things from your own library for it ("Today's
  picks from your library"); after them come the picks you saved from a
  Librarian answer. A pick that is in your library shows its **real
  cover**, and tapping it opens that item, as on the rest of Home. A pick
  you don't own (a title saved for later) shows a colored tile with its
  first letter; so does a cover that can't load. Tapping the card anywhere
  else, or a pick you don't own, opens your saved list. (A Kavita, Komga,
  Calibre, Calibre-Web or OPDS pick, or a Plex TV show, that is on none of
  Home's shelves and only in the app's library index shows its cover but
  opens the saved list.)
  The covers come from what the app already has: Home's own shelves and
  its library index, read on the device. The daily ask happens
  at most once a day, never on every visit, and a failed ask waits a day
  too; turn it off with **Settings › AI › Daily picks on Home**. See
  [What the AI can see](ai-what-it-sees.md#daily-picks-on-home).
- **New this week** — up to four things added to your libraries in the
  last 7 days, mixed across books, films and music. When the servers have
  nothing dated this week, it shows their newest additions as **Recently
  added** instead. **N more** opens all of them.

After the cards come the same shelves as before: Downloaded & Ready, Short
Listens, Unfinished Series, New from Authors You've Read, and each server's
own shelves.

### On a tablet or a computer

On the Mac, on Windows and on an iPad, the cards use the page's full
width, between the same left and right edges as the Continue cards and
the shelves:

- **Wide windows** (an iPad in landscape, most computer windows): one row
  of three — **Coming this week** with **Also in progress** under it, then
  **Your Librarian suggests**, then **New this week**.
- **Narrower windows** (an iPad in portrait, a smaller window): two
  across — Coming this week over Also in progress, beside Your Librarian
  suggests — with New this week under them, across the full width.
- **Phones** keep one card under another.
- The cards in a row are all as tall as the tallest one; their contents
  start at the top.
- When a card is hidden (in Arrange, in Kids mode, with nothing to show,
  or with no AI server), the others share the row — two side by side, or
  one across the full width. There are no empty gaps.

It depends only on how wide the window is, so resizing a window on the
Mac or Windows, or turning an iPad, changes it straight away.

## Arrange

**Arrange** (at the bottom of the side menu) is what used to be called
**Manage Sections**. It turns each part of the page on or off: the stat
pills, Continue, each of the cards above, the shared shelves and each
server's section. Under the Continue switch, **Cards shown** picks 1, 3 or
5 Continue cards.

## The side menu

Tap the menu button (or swipe in from the left edge).

### The service rail

Down the left side: **All** and a colored circle for each connected
service (Plex once, for its music and its films), with **+** at the bottom
to add a server.

- **Tap a service** to show only that service — on the Home page and on
  the library pages (Audiobooks, eBooks & Comics, Music, Podcasts). Tap it
  again, or tap **All**, to show everything.
- **Hold a service** to add it to what is shown, or to take it out, so you
  can show two or three services together.

The title at the top of the menu says what is shown: "All services", the
service's name, or how many services.

### Where you can go

- **Home** — this page.
- **Listen** — Audiobooks. With podcasts too, a **Podcasts** row sits under
  the tiles.
- **Read** — eBooks & Comics.
- **Watch** — your Plex Video or Jellyfin Video home (when you have films
  or shows). With both, the second one has its own row.
- **Music** — the Music page.
- A tile with nothing behind it in the services shown is left out.
- **Yours**: **Downloads** (with how many are downloaded), **Playlists**,
  **Upcoming** (with how many come out this week) and **Collection tools**.
- **Now**: what is playing — tap it to open the player. With nothing
  playing, the last thing you were in the middle of; tap it to resume.
- **Settings** and **Arrange** at the bottom.

## Kids mode

In Kids mode Alexandria shows the simple Kids Home instead, so this page is
not shown. If it ever is, **Coming this week**, **Upcoming** and **Your
Librarian suggests** stay hidden. See [Kids mode](kids-mode.md).

## Good to know

- Opening Home makes no requests beyond the ones it made before: the stat
  pills, Coming this week and the Librarian card use what the app already
  has saved. The one exception is the Librarian's daily picks: at most one
  request a day, to your own AI server. The picks' covers load from your
  servers like every other cover on Home.
- Free and Premium work as before; the Librarian card needs the AI
  features, which are part of Premium.
