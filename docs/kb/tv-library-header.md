# The library switch and tabs on the TV apps

On the Apple TV and on Samsung, LG and Fire TV, every place in the side menu
(**Movies**, **TV Shows**, **Music**, **Audiobooks** and **Live TV**) has the
same two rows at the top of the page:

1. The place's name, **one button naming the server and the library** you
   are looking at (for example **Plex · TV Shows**), and at the right how
   big the library is: "110 shows", "1,646 films", "312 artists", "48 books"
   or "24 channels".
2. The page's own tabs, written as plain words with a line under the one you
   are on: **Recommended · Library · Collections · Playlists** in a film,
   show or music library; **Home · Library · Series · Authors · Playlists ·
   Your Stats** in an Audiobookshelf library; the guide's sections (**All ·
   Movies · Shows · Sports · News**) on Live TV, with **Now**,
   **Recordings** and **Refresh** at the end of the row.

(Movies and TV Shows got this first. Music, Audiobooks and Live TV have it
from the Apple TV update after build 215 and the matching Samsung, LG and
Fire TV update. Before that, those three showed a pill for each server and a
second row of pills for the page's tabs.)

## Changing library or server

When a place has more than one library (for example Plex and Jellyfin
music, or two Plex servers with Live TV), choosing the place in the side
menu puts you on the library button.

- **Press** it to see every library of that place, listed under each
  server's name, with a tick beside the one you are on. Choose another to
  switch the page to it.
- **Back** (Menu on the Apple TV remote) closes the list without changing
  anything.
- **Refresh** (read the library from its server again) and **Scan Library
  Files** (Plex, for the server's owner) are at the end of the list on the
  Apple TV, with **Arrange** (move a library or a server up or down the
  list). On Samsung, LG and Fire TV, hold Select on the button for them.

With only one library in a place there is no button: the library's name
follows the place's name, and choosing the place in the side menu puts you
on the first tab.

## Moving around

- **Down** from the library button reaches the tabs; **Down** again reaches
  the page.
- **Up** goes back the same way.
- On the Apple TV, **Menu** from anywhere on the page jumps to the library
  button first; press it again for the side menu.

In a music library's **Library** tab, **Artists** and **Albums** are small
buttons under the tabs, the same as **All** and **Sort** in a film library.

## Your Stats (Audiobookshelf)

**Your Stats** is the last tab of an Audiobookshelf library. Its page opens
under the tabs, starting with **All Time** and one button for each year you
listened in.

- A year shows that year's own figures. **Books Started** and **Books
  Finished** come from Audiobookshelf's figures for that year.
- If Audiobookshelf can't answer for a year, its book counts show "—" with
  a short note. Choose the year again to try once more. (Earlier versions
  showed your all-time count of finished books under every year.)
- Years load in the background after the page opens, so switching between
  them is quick. "…" means a year is still loading.
- **All Time**'s top authors, genres and narrator come from everything you
  have listened to, not from this year. **Longest Book Finished** is shown
  for a year only.
