# Alexandria in the car (CarPlay and Android Auto)

Connect your phone to the car and open Alexandria on the car's screen.

## The tabs

- **Listen Now**: what you are in the middle of on every server, then
  recently added, most played, favourites and playlists.
- **Audiobooks** and **Music**: one page per server. With one server of
  that kind the tab opens its page; with several, you pick the server first.
- **Queue**: what is lined up to play, once something is. Cars that show
  fewer tabs reach it from **Up Next** on the Now Playing screen.

## Radio stations

Each music server's page starts with **Stations for you**: the same
stations as that server's home on your phone.

- Tap one and it plays without end. When its queue runs out, more of the
  same station follows. What each kind of station plays, and the intros
  and skits it skips: [Radio stations](radio-stations.md).
- Stations marked **AI radio** are themes your AI named on the phone. The
  car shows only themes your phone already has. It never asks the AI
  itself, so it never waits on one or adds to your AI spending.
- No stations? They are built from your media index, which the app makes
  from your libraries. Rebuild it under **You › Settings › Media index**.

## Artists

An artist's page lists every record the phone shows for them, newest first,
including records Plex files under Compilations rather than Albums.

## Going deeper (CarPlay)

CarPlay allows five screens on top of each other. When a tap would open a
sixth, Alexandria closes the deepest list first, so **Back** skips that one
step. **Up Next** on the Now Playing screen opens the Queue tab rather than
adding a screen.

## If something looks wrong

- **Nothing to show yet**: connect a server in the app first.
- **Couldn't reach** (a server name): the car cannot reach that server right
  now. Check it on your phone.
- **Not enough similar music** under a station's name: the library has too
  little music near that station. Pick another one.
