# Party volume

At a Party Play party, one slider turns the music up or down on every device in
the party at once. Each device can also have its own level, for the speaker
that is too loud in a small room.

## The party slider

**On the Remote:** under the play controls, drag **Party volume**.

**On the leading device:** the party panel has the same slider while at least
one other device follows. When the leading device plays alone, use the Remote.

The slider shows a percentage, or **Muted** at the bottom. Under it, **Set by**
names the device that moved it last. Every device in the party follows the
change within a moment.

The slider changes how loud the music plays in Alexandria. It does not change a
device's own volume buttons or system volume.

## Each device's own level

**Each device** is under the party slider, on the Remote and on the leading
device's party panel. Each device in the party has its own slider, on top of
the party volume: turn one device down and the others stay as they are, and the
party slider still moves them all. (In Alexandria 3.2.1 build 1247, Each device
is only on the Remote, and you tap it to open it.)

The leading device is listed first. A device keeps its level when it becomes
the leader.

## Which devices follow it

Phones, iPads, Macs and Windows PCs with a current Alexandria, and the Apple TV.
Devices that can't follow it, such as a watch or an older Alexandria, are listed
under **Not changed on:**.

## When it resets

The party volume and every device's level last as long as the party. They stay
when another device becomes the leader. When the party ends, the next party
starts at full volume.

## If it doesn't change

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Update Alexandria Server to change the party's volume | Your Alexandria Server is older than this feature. Update it. |
| Couldn't change the volume — try again | The change didn't reach the server. Check the connection and try again. |
| Can't change — update Alexandria on *device* | That device runs an older Alexandria. Update the app on it. |
| *Device* has left the party | The device stopped following before the change arrived. |
