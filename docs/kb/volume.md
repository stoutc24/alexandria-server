# Volume on Now Playing

From Alexandria 3.2.1 build 1250 on the phone, tablet and computer.

Every Now Playing screen, for music and for audiobooks, has a volume slider
under the play controls. What it changes depends on the device:

| Device | What the slider changes |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone and iPad | The device's volume, the same as the volume buttons. It moves when you press them. |
| Android | The device's media volume, the same as the volume buttons. It moves when you press them. |
| Mac and Windows | Alexandria's own volume, on top of the computer's volume. Alexandria remembers it. |

## At a party

The party volume and each device's own level are separate from this slider:
see [Party volume](party-volume.md). They apply only while a party is on, so
when a party is over, the slider and the volume buttons are all you need.

During a party, a following phone or computer has its own level in its party
panel, as **This device**.

## If the slider is missing

- On the iPhone simulator, and on the web, there is no slider: they have no
  device volume to show.
