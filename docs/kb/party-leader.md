# Choosing who leads a party

At a Party Play party, one device leads: it plays its queue and every other
device follows it. Any device in the party can make another one the
leader, or take the lead itself. The music doesn't stop.

## Making a device the leader

**On a phone, tablet, Mac or Windows PC in the party:** open Now Playing and
go to the **Party** tab. On a phone turned sideways, the party panel shows
next to the player. Each device that is following has **Make leader** next
to it.

- **On the leading device:** tap **Make leader** next to the device that
  should lead.
- **On a following device:** tap **Make leader** next to another follower,
  or next to your own device (marked **This phone**) to take the lead.

**On an Apple TV in the party** (Apple TV build 188 and later): in the music
player, open **Queue** and choose **Party**, next to Shuffle, Repeat and
Autoplay. Pick **Make … the leader** for another device, or **Make this Apple
TV the leader** to take the lead. Older Apple TV builds don't have the
button; choose the TV from a phone or computer instead.

Watches follow a party but can't lead it, so they never show **Make leader**.

## What happens next

The new leader takes over at once and plays on from where the party was.
The device that was leading keeps playing too, now following. It shows who
moved the lead:

- "Kate's iPhone made Living Room TV the leader", or
- "Living Room TV took the lead", when a device took the lead itself.

The new leader's queue is the party's from now on.

## If Make leader doesn't show

- **On a following device:** the companion server needs the 30 October 2026
  update or later. Before that, only the leading device can choose the
  leader.
- **Making an Apple TV the leader:** this also needs the 30 October 2026
  server update.
