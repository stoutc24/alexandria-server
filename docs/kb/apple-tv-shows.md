# TV shows on the Apple TV

Open a show from your Plex or Jellyfin library to see its seasons and
episodes.

## Where the page opens

The page opens on the season with your next episode. That's the season you're
partway through, or the one after the last season you finished. A show you
haven't started opens on Season 1. **Specials** sit at the end of the season
row.

## Play All

**Play All** (under the show's description) plays the whole show, one episode
after another, and stops after the last episode.

- It starts after the last episode you watched to the end. An episode you
  stopped partway through comes first.
- When you've watched everything, it starts again from Season 1, Episode 1.
- It leaves out the **Specials**. To watch one, open it from the Specials
  season.

## Play Season

Choose a season, then press **down** to **Play Season**. It plays that
season from its next episode.

At the end of the season, what happens next depends on **Settings › Playback ›
Auto-play next episode**:

- **On**: the next season carries on.
- **Off**: playback stops at the end of the season.

## Choosing one episode

Select an episode to play it and carry on through the season. Hold Select, or
choose the **ⓘ** button beside it, for the episode's own page.
