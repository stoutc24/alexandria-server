# The party Remote: Shuffle, Repeat and adding songs

While a Party Play party plays music, the **Remote** on a phone can turn
Shuffle and Repeat on or off for everyone, and add songs to the party's
queue.

## Shuffle and Repeat

1. Open the party's **Remote**.
2. Tap **Shuffle**, at the left end of the play controls, to turn it on or
   off for the whole party. The song that's playing keeps playing; only the
   songs after it change order.
3. Tap **Repeat**, at the right end of the play controls, to step through
   **Repeat off**, **Repeat all** and **Repeat one**:
   - **Repeat all** starts the queue again after the last song.
   - **Repeat one** plays the current song again.

The device leading the party makes the change, and everyone in the party
hears it. The buttons show the party's settings, not your phone's own
player. A leading Apple TV makes the change too (Apple TV build 192 and
later).

### If a button isn't there

- **Your Alexandria Server needs an update.** Shuffle and Repeat on the
  Remote need the 1 November 2026 server update or later.
- **The leading device can't do it.** An audiobook has neither, and Shuffle
  needs more than one song in the queue.
- **The party has ended.**

### Messages under the controls

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *Device* can't change shuffle — update Alexandria on it | The leading device runs an older Alexandria. Update the app on it. The same goes for "can't change repeat". |
| *Device* is asleep and can't change this from here | Wake the leading device, open Alexandria on it, and try again. |
| Nobody is leading the party right now | Start playing on one of the party's devices. |
| *Device* needs an Alexandria update for the Remote | Update the app on the leading device. |
| Update Alexandria Server to change shuffle and repeat | Your Alexandria Server is older than this feature. Update it. |
| Couldn't reach the party — try again | Check your connection and tap again. |
| The party couldn't change that — try again | The leading device didn't take the change. Tap again. |

A message clears after a few seconds.

## Adding songs

1. On the Remote, find **Up next**.
2. Tap **+ Add songs**.
3. Search for a song or album, or pick from your albums.
4. Tap the add button next to a song or album, then choose:
   - **Play Next**: plays right after the song that's playing.
   - **Add to Party Queue**: goes at the end of the queue.
5. The songs appear under **Up next** on the Remote.

**+ Add songs** shows when the party is playing Plex music, your phone is
signed in to a Plex server, and the leading device takes queue changes. For
now, only Plex songs can join a party's queue. To take a song out, tap **✕**
next to it; see [Adding and removing songs at a party](party-queue.md).
