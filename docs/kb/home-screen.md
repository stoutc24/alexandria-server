# Choosing your home screen

On a phone, tablet or computer, Alexandria can open one service's own home
instead of the unified view of all your servers.

## Pick the home

1. Open **Settings › Home Screen**.
2. Under **Default Home Service**, pick where the app opens.

The choice applies when the app shows one service at a time (not the unified
view).

Every home the service switcher offers is in the list, including:

- **Plex Music** and **Plex Video**
- **Jellyfin** and **Jellyfin Video**
- Audiobookshelf, Kavita, Storyteller, Grimmory, Calibre, Calibre-Web,
  Komga, Navidrome, OPDS and Stump

Only services signed in on this device are listed. **First configured** lets
the app pick.

## Good to know

- The list appears once this device has more than one home to choose from.
  Plex alone counts as two (Music and Video).
- The choice is kept on this device only.
- If you chose Plex before this list had Plex Video, the app still opens
  Plex Music.
