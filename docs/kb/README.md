# Alexandria help articles

Plain how-to pages for people using the Alexandria app. Each matches what the
app shows today, with menu paths and button labels as they appear on screen.
They are updated with every app release that changes what they describe. For
installing and running the Alexandria companion server itself, see the
[README](../../README.md).

## Check on a real device before publishing

These were proven in tests and simulators, not yet on hardware:

- [ ] App icon (phone after 1272, Apple TV after 214): proven in tests,
      in builds for iPhone, Mac and Android, on the Apple TV simulator (a
      real switch, its OK, the Home screen icon, and back) and on the
      iPhone simulator (iOS took the switch and showed its notice with the
      new icon; a test cannot press its OK). Still to check on real
      devices: the iPhone's and iPad's Home Screen icon and its notice;
      the Apple TV Home screen icon after an upload passes App Store
      validation; an Android phone's launcher (Pixel and Samsung) — the
      new icon, whether it closes the app, the home-screen icon, the
      long-press shortcuts and the widgets after a switch; the Mac Dock
      icon; the Windows window and taskbar icon (Windows builds only in
      CI).

- [ ] Upcoming releases' Movies, TV and Music tabs (phone after 1272):
      proven in tests against stand-in answers shaped like TVmaze's,
      MusicBrainz's and TMDB's (TVmaze and MusicBrainz checked against a
      few live answers for famous titles; TMDB from its documentation only,
      no key). Still to check on a real device: the Movies tab with the
      app's TMDB key in the build; real Plex and Jellyfin libraries (the
      ids Plex gives with `includeGuids`, the most-recently-watched order);
      the first Music check's length; Open show / movie / artist on each
      server; the dates in a country other than the US.

- [ ] Chapters (phone after 1268, Apple TV after 211, Samsung and Fire TV):
      proven against the test server only. Still to check: a real Plex and
      Jellyfin film with chapter thumbnails on each app, and the Apple TV's
      pictures arriving mid-play without disturbing the player.

- [ ] Premium free trial (stores live 2026-11-11; Premium screen wording
      from phone 1268): the week-free line and Start free week on an
      iPhone or Mac whose Apple Account never had Premium, the normal
      price for one that did, the same on Android; and the iPhone's
      RevenueCat paywall (its trial text is set in RevenueCat).

- [ ] Profile looks (Apple TV after 208, Samsung and Fire TV, phone after
      1265; server 1.1.0+): proven in tests, on the Apple TV simulator
      (tvOS 26.5 and 27.2) and in a desktop browser against a stand-in
      server. Still to check: a look following a person from a TV to a
      phone and back through the real server; text size and fonts on real
      TVs; readability of white button labels on light accent colors on
      the phone.

- [ ] Marking watched and unwatched (phone after 1263, Apple TV after 206,
      Samsung and Fire TV): proven in tests, on the Apple TV simulator and
      in a desktop browser, against a test server only. Still to check on
      real devices and a real Plex and a real Jellyfin: a film and an
      episode both ways; that marking a season or a whole show marks every
      episode under it (assumed from how Plex's and Jellyfin's own apps
      do it, never measured); Continue Watching and Next Up afterwards;
      a shared user's marks staying their own.

- [ ] Video volume, subtitles Off, scanning and refreshing (phone 1262 and
      1263, Apple TV 206, Samsung and Fire TV): proven in tests, on the
      simulators and against a test server; one real scan of one Plex
      library was measured. Still to check on real devices: the speaker,
      slider, keys and wheel on a Mac and a Windows PC; the speaker's
      slider on an iPhone and an Android phone; subtitles staying off
      through a whole episode; the ⋮ scan on a phone signed in as the
      server's owner and as a shared user (who must not see it); the hold
      menus and Server Admin on a real TV.

- [ ] Alexandria Link (phone, Apple TV, Samsung and Fire TV; server from
      8 November 2026): proven against a test server on loopback (a code
      approved as a person brought that person's services; a code without a
      key brought none). Still to check on real devices: a phone and an
      Apple TV linked to the real server, Plex as a picked person on a real
      Plex, and a sign-out from the server removing what the link added.

- [ ] Stations in search (phone 1256, Apple TV 201, Samsung and Fire TV):
      proven against a test server, where a playlist station played four
      artists on Plex and three on Jellyfin. Still to check:
      - an artist station from search on a real library with similar
        artists (the test server's artists share nothing, so it said "Not
        enough similar music");
      - a Navidrome station (no test server; only canned replies), and
        whether it needs the server's Last.fm connection;
      - a Jellyfin Instant Mix on a real Jellyfin, on the TV apps.

- [ ] Browsing films and shows (phone 1255, Apple TV 199): the Apple TV
      pages and filters were proven in the simulator against a real Plex
      server (Genre › Adventure gave the same 779 films Plex counts); the
      phone pages against a test server only. Check the phone's shelves,
      tabs and filters on a real Plex library.

- [ ] Party volume and announcements (both pages): written from the agreed
      design and the server alone, ahead of the app builds. Check every
      label and message against the shipped phone and Apple TV apps, then
      in a room: the party slider and each device's level, a message and
      live voice heard together on every speaker (server added 2026-10-31;
      proven on a test server over loopback, no app yet).
- [ ] Announcements on Android phones (build 1247): a message and live voice
      play on time with the music dipping, including through a Bluetooth
      speaker (proven on the Android emulator only, with the output forced
      to 44.1 kHz for the Bluetooth case).
- [ ] The party Remote's Shuffle, Repeat and + Add songs with a phone and an
      Apple TV leading (phone 1247, Apple TV 192; proven on the party rig and
      in the simulators).
- [ ] One Apple TV following another (Apple TV 192): in step at every song
