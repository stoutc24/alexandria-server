# Your look: colors, background, font and text size

Each person can give Alexandria their own look: a profile color, an accent
color for buttons and highlights, a background, a font and a text size. On
a TV each profile has its own look, and switching profiles switches it.

## Where to change it

- **Apple TV:** **Settings › Profile › Look**.
- **Samsung, LG and Fire TV:** **Settings › Profile › Look**.
- **Phone, tablet and computer:** **You › Settings › Appearance**. The look
  sits with the app's other appearance settings.

Every choice shows at once, so you can try them.

## What you can change

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Profile color | 12 colors. Your circle on "Who's listening?", in the corner of the TV apps and on the You tab. |
| Accent color | The app's own color, or one of 12. Buttons, icons and highlighted lines. |
| Background | Midnight (the usual), Black, Charcoal, Forest, Plum or Ocean. On a phone it applies in dark mode. |
| Now Playing background | On the TV apps: the TV's own setting, None, Cover Colors, Fireplace, Rain, Snowfall, Library or Starfield. |
| Font | Default, Rounded or Serif. |
| Text size | Normal, Large or Extra Large. |

Every color and background was checked so text stays readable.

## Where your look is kept

- **If your device is linked to your Alexandria companion server as you,**
  your look is kept on the server and follows you: change it on the TV and
  your phone and other TVs pick it up the next time they check, usually
  within a minute. A change made while the server can't be reached is sent
  when it can.
- **Otherwise,** the look is kept on that device (on a TV, for that
  profile).

The Look page says which applies. A companion server from before this
feature keeps nothing: the look stays on each device until the server is
updated.

On a phone, a few choices exist only on that phone (theme presets, the
other custom colors, Small text, a custom background color). Picking one
of those keeps it on that phone, until you or another of your devices
changes that part of your look again.

## Kids

In Kids mode a child can pick **My Color** on Kids Home: their own profile
color, from 12. Nothing else about the look can be changed in Kids mode; a
grown-up sets the rest from their own profile or before turning Kids mode
on.

## Good to know

- **Text size on the Apple TV:** tvOS 26 does not let apps change text
  size, so on those Apple TVs the choice is kept but has no effect (the
  Look page says so). On tvOS 27 most text grows. Some text in fixed sizes,
  such as large titles, stays the same everywhere.
- **Rounded font:** it is used where the device has a rounded font. Most
  TVs and phones don't, and then Rounded looks like Default.
- **The focus highlight** on the TV apps stays white, so you can always see
  where you are.
