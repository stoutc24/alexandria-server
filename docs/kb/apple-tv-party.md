# Following a party on the Apple TV

When another device plays music in a Party Play party (an iPhone, an iPad, a
computer or another Apple TV), your Apple TV can play along in step, so every
room hears the same song at the same moment.

## Joining from Home

1. Open Home. While a party is playing, a card near the top says where, for
   example "Playing on Living Room TV".
2. Choose **Join & Play**.

Your Apple TV starts the same song at the same point. When a song ends, the
next one starts on every device together.

## Joining from the music player

If your Apple TV is playing its own music while another device leads a party,
the player says "*device* is leading — playing here". Choose **Join party** in
the row of buttons under the controls. Your music stops, and your Apple TV
joins the party's song in step (Apple TV build 192 and later).

## Joining at the very end of a song

If you join in the last moments of a song, your Apple TV waits for the
party's next song and starts with it a few seconds later. It doesn't play the
end of the old song.

## When the leading Apple TV picks other music

If the Apple TV that leads the party starts another song, an album or a
station, it stays the leader. Every device following it switches to the new
music too.

## When the leader stops or goes away

- **Stop on the leading Apple TV:** the party keeps going. A device that was
  following takes the lead and plays on.
- **The leader loses power or its connection:** the devices following it
  finish the song they are on, then wait. About a minute and a half after the
  leader went quiet, one of them takes the party over and plays on.

## While you follow

Shuffle and Repeat belong to the leader. Its choices apply to everyone in the
party.

**Tip:** give each Apple TV its own name in **Settings › Companion Server ›
Device name**, so you can tell which one is leading. Two Apple TVs both called
"Apple TV" look the same in the party.

Both Apple TVs need build 192 or later for one Apple TV to follow another.
