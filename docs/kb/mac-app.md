# The Mac and Windows apps and wide windows

## Scrolling with a mouse or a trackpad (Mac and Windows)

- **Drag with the mouse** to scroll any list, as you would with a finger.
  Drag a row of covers left or right to see more of it.
- **Refresh a page:** at the top of the page, drag down with the mouse and
  let go.
- **The mouse wheel** scrolls the page. To scroll a row of covers sideways
  with the wheel, point at the row and hold **Shift** while you turn it.
- **A trackpad:** swipe with two fingers in any direction. To refresh, swipe
  down past the top of the page (on a Mac, keep going a little further).

## Closing the window keeps the music playing

Like other Mac music apps:

- Click the red close button and the window goes away; what is playing keeps
  playing.
- Click Alexandria's icon in the Dock to bring the window back.
- To quit completely, choose **Alexandria › Quit Alexandria** or press ⌘Q.
- A video pauses when you close the window, since nobody is watching it.

## Now Playing in the menu bar

What Alexandria plays appears in the Mac's **Now Playing** in the menu bar
and Control Center, with the cover, and can be paused and skipped from
there while Alexandria is in the background.

## The sidebar and the docked player (Mac and iPad)

On a wide window — the Mac, or an iPad wide enough — Alexandria shows a
sidebar instead of the bottom bar:

- **Home, Library, Search, You**, then your servers, then **Add a server**
  and **Ask the Librarian**.
- What is playing sits in a strip along the bottom of the page area, under
  every page. Pages end above it, so it never covers a button. Click it to
  open Now Playing.
- The strip steps aside for Now Playing, for readers and video (they take the
  whole window), and while the on-screen keyboard is up on an iPad.

## How pages fit a wide window (Mac, Windows and iPad)

- **Now Playing:** the player and its tabs (Up Next, Lyrics, Artist, Party)
  sit together in the middle of the window, however wide it is. In a party,
  **Make leader** sits next to the device it belongs to.
- **Album and book pages:** the buttons and the list stop at a comfortable
  width on the left. On the Mac and Windows each song is one line; its
  buttons appear when the pointer is over it, and a right-click opens its
  menu. An iPad keeps finger-size rows.
- **Homes:** on the Audiobookshelf Home, Continue Playing cards sit two
  across. On the All servers Home, the Continue card and its service switch
  stay a comfortable width.
- **Search:** the filters and results sit in a column in the middle.
