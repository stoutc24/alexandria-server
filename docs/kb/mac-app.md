# The Mac app and wide windows

## Closing the window keeps the music playing

Like other Mac music apps:

- Click the red close button and the window goes away; what is playing keeps
  playing.
- Click Alexandria's icon in the Dock to bring the window back.
- To quit completely, choose **Alexandria › Quit Alexandria** or press ⌘Q.
- A video pauses when you close the window, since nobody is watching it.

## Now Playing in the menu bar

What Alexandria plays appears in the Mac's **Now Playing** in the menu bar
and Control Center, with the cover, and can be paused and skipped from
there while Alexandria is in the background.

## The sidebar and the docked player (Mac and iPad)

On a wide window — the Mac, or an iPad wide enough — Alexandria shows a
sidebar instead of the bottom bar:

- **Home, Library, Search, You**, then your servers, then **Add a server**
  and **Ask the Librarian**.
- What is playing sits in a strip along the bottom of the page area, under
  every page. Pages end above it, so it never covers a button. Click it to
  open Now Playing.
- The strip steps aside for Now Playing, for readers and video (they take the
  whole window), and while the on-screen keyboard is up on an iPad.
