# Alexandria on the Apple TV Home screen (Top Shelf)

When Alexandria sits in the **top row** of the Apple TV Home screen, the area
above the icons shows Alexandria's own rows while its icon is highlighted:

- **Continue:** what you are partway through (audiobooks, films, episodes),
  with a bar showing how far along each one is.
- **Recently Added:** what is new on your servers.

Select a poster to open it in Alexandria.

## A film in more than one library

Plex can hold the same film in several libraries (say **All Movies** and
**Comedies**). Alexandria shows it once, on these rows and on its own Home
(Continue Watching, Recently Added): Continue keeps the copy you watched
last, and Recently Added the newest. If you hide a library from Home, a
film that another library still holds stays on Home. (Apple TV builds
before 198 showed a poster for every copy.)

## More than one person on the Apple TV

The rows show what the last person to use Alexandria was watching. When the
Apple TV has more than one profile (or a profile with a PIN), selecting a
poster asks **Who's listening?** first, and the PIN if that profile has one.
The poster then opens for the person chosen, if their profile has the server
it came from. If it doesn't, Alexandria opens on their own Home instead.
(Apple TV builds before 197 opened the poster on top of the question,
signed in as the last person.)

## Getting it to show

1. Put Alexandria in the top row. Highlight its icon, hold the clickpad
   center (or Select), choose **Move**, move it up, and press again.
2. Open Alexandria once. It fills the rows from its own Home page, and
   refreshes them every time Home refreshes.

If the area shows only the Alexandria logo, open the app once and go back
to the Home screen. (Apple TV builds 187 to 189 could not save the rows on
a real Apple TV, so they showed only the logo; build 190 fixes that.)

## What the rows keep

The rows hold only each item's title, how far along you are, and a copy of
its poster saved on the Apple TV. No server address, password or sign-in
key is stored with them. What a poster opens stays inside Alexandria.
