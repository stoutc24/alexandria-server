# Announcements at a party

Talk to every room in a Party Play party at once. The music dips on every
device, a chime plays, and then your voice plays on every speaker at the same
moment. When you finish, the music comes back up.

## Message or Live

Open **Announcements** on the Remote, or tap **Announce** on the party panel.
Choose **Message** or **Live**.

**Message:** hold the microphone button and speak, then let go to send. A
message can be up to 1 minute; a countdown shows near the end, and it sends
itself at the limit. It plays on every device a moment after you let go. To
cancel, slide your finger away before you let go.

**Live:** hold the button and wait for **Speak now**, then talk. Your voice
plays about a second after you speak. Hold the phone close to your mouth, away
from the speakers. Let go to stop. Live can last up to 2 minutes.

On a Mac or Windows PC, click the button to start and click it again to finish.

## Who hears it

The line under the button says **Heard on:** and lists the devices that will
play it. Devices listed under **Not on:** need an Alexandria update.

- A message also plays on your own device when it is playing the party.
- Live voice never plays on your own device, so you don't hear yourself a
  second late. Your device's music still dips.
- A phone on the Remote, a watch, and a device in a phone call don't play
  announcements.

## Letting other devices announce

The leading device has a switch: **Let other devices make announcements**. Turn
it off and only the leading device can announce. The switch belongs to the
device that set it: if another device becomes the leader, everyone can announce
again.

An Apple TV has no microphone and no announcement switch. While an Apple TV
leads, everyone can announce. When your device leads again, its switch applies
again.

## Stopping one

**Stop announcement** appears for the leading device and for the device that
sent it. The music comes back up on every device.

## Limits

- One announcement at a time.
- Up to 6 announcements a minute.
- Messages up to 1 minute; live up to 2 minutes.

## Your voice stays private

Your voice goes through your own Alexandria Server and nowhere else. The server
keeps it in memory only — never on its disk, never in a log — and deletes it
within 30 seconds of it playing, or as soon as the party ends. Only the devices
in your own party can get it. Alexandria uses the microphone only while you hold
the button.

## Messages you might see

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *Device* is announcing — try again in a moment | Another announcement is playing. Wait for it to finish. |
| Only *device* can make announcements right now | The leading device turned off **Let other devices make announcements**. |
| Too many announcements — wait a minute | More than 6 in a minute. |
| The server is busy — try again in a moment | Your Alexandria Server is handling as many announcements as it can. |
| Keep holding while you speak | The message was too short. Hold the button the whole time you speak. |
| Couldn't send the announcement — try again | It didn't reach the server. Tap **Send again**. |
| Slow connection — some words may not be heard | Live voice is arriving late. Words that arrive too late are skipped. |
| *Device* stopped the announcement | The leading device or the sender stopped it. |
| Alexandria needs the microphone to make announcements. | Allow the microphone in Settings. **Open Settings** takes you there. |
| Update Alexandria Server to make announcements | Your Alexandria Server is older than this feature. Update it. |
