# Recently Played and Top Artists in Plex Music

From Alexandria 3.2.1 build 1250 on the phone, tablet and computer, and
Apple TV build 195.

## Every device you play on

**Recently Played** and **Top Artists** on the Plex Music home come from your
Plex account's listening history, so a song played on your computer shows on
your phone too. Before build 1250, the phone, tablet and computer apps
showed only what that one device had played.

- **Recently Played**: albums, the latest play first.
- **Top Artists**: the artists you play most, from your last 400 plays.

The Apple TV has read Plex's history since it added these shelves. From
build 195 it keeps only your own plays, as the other apps do.

## When a song shows up

Plex adds a song to your history when it has played to (nearly) its end. The
phone, tablet and computer apps also count a song once you have heard 30
seconds of it on that device. So:

- A song still playing, or one you skipped early, shows only on the device
  that played it.
- A song played on another device shows the next time you open Plex Music,
  or straight away when you pull down to refresh.

## Whose plays

Only your own. If others in your Plex Home have their own Plex sign-in, they
see their own plays, and you never see theirs. A Plex Home user who plays
music through your sign-in shares your history, because Plex records those
plays as yours.

## Data

Each time you open Plex Music, Alexandria asks Plex only for the plays since
the last time it looked. That is one small request, on Wi-Fi or cellular.
After a long session on another device, the full history (400 plays) downloads
again on Wi-Fi. On cellular, only the newest plays download.
