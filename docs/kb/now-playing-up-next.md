# Up Next on the Now Playing screen

Open Now Playing (tap the mini player), then scroll up past the cover and
controls to the tabs. **Up Next** is the first tab: the whole queue, in the
order it plays.

## Where you are in the queue

The song playing now is highlighted, and it is the top row of the list.
Songs you have already heard are above it: scroll up to see them.

- **Scrolling from the player into the list**: when your scroll stops, the
  list glides to the playing song.
- **When the next song starts** while the list is on screen, the list
  glides on to it. If your finger is on the list at that moment, it waits
  until you let go.
- **iPad and Mac, side by side**: the list sits beside the player and
  opens on the playing song.

Near the end of the queue the list can't scroll any further, so the
playing song sits lower down.

## Changing the queue

- **Tap** a song to play it now.
- **Swipe left** on a song to remove it.
- **Drag the handle** on the right of a song to move it.

Following someone else's Party Play, the list shows the party's queue. Tap
a song to ask the party to play it, or tap **✕** to take it out. Moving songs
is up to the device that leads. See
[Adding and removing songs at a party](party-queue.md).

## A party on a big screen

On Windows, the Mac and the iPad, Now Playing during a party keeps the whole
player: the controls on one side, and **Up Next**, **Lyrics**, **Artist** and
**Party** tabs on the other. The party view that replaces the player is only
for a phone turned sideways.

## The Artist tab

The tab next to **Lyrics** shows the playing song's artist. Tap the
artist's picture or name (marked with **›**) to close Now Playing and open
their page. On Plex, **Artists you may like** below opens those artists
the same way. A Jellyfin song carries only its artist's name, so the app
looks the artist up first: when your Jellyfin has no artist by that name,
there is no **›**.
