# Premium free trial

Alexandria Premium Monthly and Annual start with **one week free**. You pay
nothing during that week; after it, the plan you picked renews at its normal
price unless you cancel.

## Who can get it

- One free week per person, once. On the App Store it counts across both
  plans: someone who already had a Premium trial, or a Premium
  subscription, isn't offered another one. On Google Play it's for people
  who have never subscribed to Premium.
- Lifetime is a one-time purchase and has no trial.
- The Premium screen only says "1 week free" to someone who can still get
  it. Everyone else sees the normal price.

## Where

- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV:** through the App Store.
- **Android:** through Google Play.
- **Windows:** Premium is sold through the Microsoft Store, which has no
  trial yet.
- **Samsung, LG and Fire TV:** not on those stores yet.

## How to start it

Open **Premium** (You › Settings › Alexandria Premium, or Settings ›
Alexandria Premium on the Apple TV) and choose Monthly or Annual. When
you can get the trial, the plan says "1 week free, then" its price (on
Android and the Mac its button reads **Start free week**). The store's own
sheet confirms the free week and the price after it.

## How to cancel

Cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends and you won't be charged:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Alexandria.
- **Mac:** App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions.
- **Apple TV:** Settings › Users and Accounts › Subscriptions.
- **Android:** Google Play › your profile › Payments and subscriptions ›
  Subscriptions › Alexandria.

Premium stays on until the end of the free week after you cancel.
