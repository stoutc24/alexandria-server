# Chapters in films and shows

When a film or episode on your Plex or Jellyfin server has chapters, the
video player lists them so you can jump straight to one.

## Where to find them

- **Apple TV:** while a video plays, swipe down on the remote. The
  **Chapters** row is beside Info, Audio & Video and Details. The chapter
  you're watching is marked; pick another to jump there.
- **Samsung, LG and Fire TV:** press Down while a video plays to open the
  panel, then choose **Chapters**. The chapter you're watching says
  "Playing now". The progress bar shows a mark where each chapter starts.
- **Phone, tablet and computer:** tap **Chapters** in the player's bottom
  row. On a phone the list slides up from the bottom; on a wider window it
  opens at the side. The seek bar shows a mark where each chapter starts.

## Pictures

Each chapter shows a picture when your server made one:

- **Plex:** Settings › Library › "Generate chapter thumbnails".
- **Jellyfin:** the library's "Extract chapter images" option, or the
  scheduled task of that name.

A chapter without a picture shows its name and start time on a plain
card. A chapter without a name is listed as "Chapter 1", "Chapter 2" and
so on.

## Good to know

- Chapters appear when a video has at least two.
- The chapter list comes from your server: if a file has no chapters,
  nothing is shown.
- On the Apple TV, the pictures load a moment after the video starts.
  The Apple TV's progress bar does not show chapter marks.
