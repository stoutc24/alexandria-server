# Choosing Alexandria's app icon

Alexandria comes with nine icons: the original and eight variations of it
(fuller, more vivid, a night sky, sapphire, a lantern glow, warm amber,
sapphire night, and the lighthouse and book without the word). Every icon
is free.

## Where to change it

- **iPhone and iPad:** **Settings › Appearance & Home › App icon**.
- **Android phones and tablets:** **Settings › Appearance & Home › App icon**.
- **Mac and Windows:** **Settings › Appearance & Home › App icon**.
- **Apple TV:** **Settings › App Icon**.

Tap or select an icon to use it. The one in use has a tick. Pick
**Original** to go back to the usual icon.

## What changes on each device

| Device | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone and iPad | The app's icon on the Home Screen and wherever iOS shows it. iOS shows a short message with the new icon: tap OK. |
| Apple TV | The icon on the Home screen. tvOS shows a short message with the new icon: select OK. It's the same for every profile on that Apple TV. |
| Android | The icon in the app list and on the home screen. Some home screens close Alexandria for a moment while the icon changes, or take it off the home screen: add it back from the app list. |
| Mac | The icon in the Dock while Alexandria is open. Finder, Launchpad and the Dock (when Alexandria isn't open) keep the installed icon. Alexandria puts your choice back each time it opens. |
| Windows | Alexandria's window icon while it's open. The Start menu keeps the installed icon. Alexandria puts your choice back each time it opens. |

Each device keeps its own choice. Changing the icon on your phone doesn't
change it on your Apple TV or computer.

## Not available

Samsung, LG and Fire TV don't let an app change its own icon, so they
always show the original.
