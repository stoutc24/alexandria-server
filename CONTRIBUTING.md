# Contributing

Alexandria Server is **proprietary software** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
The source is published so you can see what runs on your machine. It is
not open source.

- **Code contributions are not accepted.** Pull requests are closed
  automatically.
- **Forks may not be redistributed.** You may not publish modified copies
  or images built from them (LICENSE, section 2).

## How you can help

- **Report a bug.** [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick
  **Bug report**.
- **Ask for a feature.** [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and
  pick **Feature request**.
- **Talk with us and other users** on Reddit:
  [r/AlexandriaMediaLib2](https://www.reddit.com/r/AlexandriaMediaLib2/).
- **Email** stoutservers@gmail.com.

**Security problems:** please do not open a public issue. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
